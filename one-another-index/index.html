How this index works

What's included

Passages that teach or show how believers should treat one another, handle conflict, and relate to unbelievers, opponents and governing authorities. Stories count, including cautionary ones, and so do household and workplace instructions. Passages about doctrine, the end times or private devotion are left out unless the text itself draws a point about how to treat people.

Each passage carries one to three topics, so many appear under more than one. Key passages are the ones a group leader should know first, and each topic lists them at the top.

About the proverbs

Proverbs is a selection, not the whole book: only sayings that speak directly to relationships, conflict and outsiders, chosen for small-group use. They sit in their own section under each topic, after the New Testament. Proverbs describe how life usually works. They are wisdom rather than promises, and some pull against each other on purpose, like 26:4–5. Where the New Testament quotes or echoes a proverb, the two link to each other.

Using it with your group

Start from a situation or a topic, tap a passage to see its summary, and save the ones you want to discuss. Then open Saved and copy the list into your group chat. Every reference in the copied list links to the passage on ESV.org. Your saved list stays on this device.

About the text

Scripture opens in the English Standard Version at ESV.org, published by Crossway. References follow ESV verse numbering. The titles and summaries here are study aids written for this index, not Scripture, so read each passage itself before teaching from it.
